DESCRIPTION
A client asked me to remove one question from a booking form. My AI tool found it was still driving the admin page: a paw icon, the cleaner assignment and a pet fee. I asked the client four questions. They said: "Just paste my email into Claude. It'll handle it."

The AI can find where a field is used. It can't know why you added it or what you want now. This version also shows what good answers sound like, the kind of email your AI can actually work with.

Before you remove any field, paste this into your AI tool:

Before removing the "[question]" field, list every page, rule, calculation and email that reads or displays it. Don't change anything yet.

Then answer the why yourself, in plain words, and give those answers to your AI with the request.

The app and the details in this story are a made-up stand-in for a real review.
