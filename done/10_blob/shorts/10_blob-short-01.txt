TITLE
I clicked post once. My app deleted everything.

DESCRIPTION
No hacker, no typo. This app kept every post in one database row, so a single bad save wiped the whole board. The fix: give every post its own row.

Full fix: https://youtu.be/4x5w0DcKOPw
